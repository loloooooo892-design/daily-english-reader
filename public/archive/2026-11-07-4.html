The Best Early Black Friday TV Deals

You should never buy a TV at full price. This is a hill I will die on, and I am proven right every year when I see 20-, 30-, and even 50-percent-off deals on some of the best TVs. Even though Black Friday is weeks away, I'm already seeing some great TV deals available now.

If you're in the market for a new screen, now is a smart time to shop options of all sizes, from big, premium OLED TVs for a home theater to small art TVs for the bedroom. Because you'll be ahead of the deal-hunting masses, you probably won't have to worry about shipping delays or out-of-stock notifications. A WIRED reviewer has tried every TV featured below (with one exception, the LG C6, which is currently in testing).

For more great discounts, check out our Best Early Black Friday Deals, as well as early discounts on smart bird feeders, mattresses, and espresso machines.

LG

C6 OLED TV

This is the lowest price I've ever seen for the 65-inch LG C6, a fantastic deal on an excellent OLED TV. LG's C-Series TVs are consistently among our top picks due to their high brightness, uniform blacks, and vivid, natural colors. Add in their ample connectivity, which makes them versatile enough for gamers, and it's clear why this is a top OLED pick.

The premium 2026 Samsung S95H OLED TV is now at its lowest price yet. WIRED reviewer John Brandon praised the TV for its accurate color and deep blacks. New to the latest S95H model is a silver bezel that’s recessed behind the main OLED panel, making the TV look like a metal picture frame.

B6

I understand wanting a nice OLED TV but not wanting to pay premium OLED prices. That's where budget OLEDs like the LG B6 shine—especially when steeply discounted. The 48-inch version, in particular, is 50 percent off at Best Buy right now, which is such a good deal, I thought it was a typo. (I won’t be surprised if the price mysteriously goes up.) Brandon praised the TV's thin size, simple setup, and deep and rich blacks.
