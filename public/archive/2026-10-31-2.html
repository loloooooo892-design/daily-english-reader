And how to use Espers in battle

Final Fantasy Resonance's Espers give Fina an extra edge in combat, or sometimes they're just convenient decoys to bait the enemy into attacking them and not your party. However you use them, Espers are definitely worth the MP it costs to summon them. You have to put in some extra work to obtain them, though. Espers are tucked away in different spots around Resonance's world, and you have to find and often fight them before Fina can use their powers.

Below, we show you where to find Esper locations in Final Fantasy Resonance and explain how to recruit them.

Update (Oct. 30): We've updated this guide's table with Bahamut and an in-game image for Shiva's location.

How Espers work in Final Fantasy Resonance

Espers look like traditional Final Fantasy summons, but they behave differently. Fina is the only party member in Final Fantasy Resonance who can summon Espers, and there's an MP cost for each summon. They remain on the field until they act three times, but while all your support spells apply to them, you can't control them. The Esper will use a different action from their arsenal each turn and then end their time on the field with their strongest skill. You can't decide which skill your Esper uses or which enemy it targets.

Once the Esper vanishes, you can have Fina summon them again on her next turn and repeat the process until she doesn't have enough MP for the summon. MP is the only thing that matters. There's no charge meter or timer dictating how often you can bring an Esper out.

All Esper locations in Final Fantasy Resonance

Espers typically live in caves or dungeons, and you can usually pick up details about their locations by chatting with people in town. The knowledgeable people will have exclamation marks over their heads, indicating they know something worth telling. That info will then make its way to the world map, so you can bring it up and see that Esper's location any time.

Which is handy, since some of the Espers you have to fight are more challenging than the mandatory bosses that show up in that chapter. If you're running into difficulties, it's not a problem to just nope out and come back once your level is higher. The Esper will still be there.

First available chapter

Recruitment requirement

Siren

Chapter 1

Siren is a mandatory boss, so this is unmissable. She'll join you after you beat her in battle.

Ramuh

Chapter 2

Find Ramuh in the Mt. Thunderclap summit in Dirnado (use the entrance east of Zidane's shrine) and defeat him in battle. Ramuh is level 20 and only weak to earth.

Carbuncle

Chapter 3

Read the riddle on the tablet in Ruby Cave. You need to find three items on the world map in a specific order to recruit Carbuncle. However, the game marks their locations on the mini-map, and you can only find them in the correct order.

Ifrit

Chapter 5

After you get the airship, you'll need to fly into this volcano to battle Ifrit. You may learn the rumor about Ifrit before you can actually fly up to him. As you may expect from the fire-based summon, he is weak to water. He's level 33.

Titan

Chapter 5

You'll learn about this one in Kol, long before you can access it. Titan lives in the giant rock east of Siren's tower, but you can only bust the rock open after clearing the first round of gladiator trials in Grandshelt during Chapter 5. You earn a new hammer for Lid that way, and she uses it to break the rock.

You'll need to fight Titan after breaking the rock. He's level 30 and weak to wind.

Leviathan

Chapter 5

After unlocking the airship, Nichol will have a side quest in Olderion called "Nichol's Duty." You'll unlock Leviathan upon completing this quest.

Shiva

Chapter 5

You can find this ice queen after finding Mysidia. She's in a snowstorm on the southern island. You will need to fight her. She's level 35 and weak to fire.

Odin

Chapter 6

After Fina learns Holy, head east in Gronoa to the Bladerain Stele cave and interact with the tablet to start this quest. During the quest, you'll need to fight Odin, who is level 45 and weak to electricity.

Bahamut

Chapter 7

Obtain the other Espers and clear Mt. Raizen in Chapter 7. When you board your airship, a cutscene with Fina will trigger. Talk to Evan in order to head to Bahamut for a battle. Bahamut is level 50 and has no weaknesses or elemental resistances.
