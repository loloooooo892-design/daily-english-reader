Dermstore Coupons: 25% Off for 2026

You can trust what you buy at Dermstore. Unlike Sephora and Ulta, Dermstore focuses on dermatologist-tested brands, like EltaMD, SkinCeuticals, and SkinMedica—many of which you won’t find at these other stores. Also, Dermstore doesn’t just sell skincare; the retailer carries beauty tools and wellness gadgets, from LED masks to Theraguns and sex toys. And many of these products are FSA/HSA eligible.

Big sales may be occasional, but Dermstore still knows how to treat its customers. Exclusive offers, free standard shipping, and a loyalty program all make shopping here worthwhile. Its BeautyFix subscription is also a fun perk: you get five dermatologist-backed full- and deluxe-size formulas each month (over $100 in value) for just $21.95-$24.95 a box, with free shipping after your first order. Read on below for some of the best Dermstore promo codes and deals we’ve found so far.

Unlock Your First Dermstore Promo Code: Save 15% on Your First Order

New customers can take 15% off their first order just by signing up for the Dermstore email newsletter or SMS alerts. Select items are excluded from this promotion.

Claim 5% Back on Every Purchase With Dermstore Rewards

As a Dermstore Rewards member, I love that I earn points on every Dermstore purchase—five points for every $1 spent, to be exact. That’s 5% cash back on every order. Just make sure you’re logged in when you check out. And unlike the welcome offer, there are zero brand exclusions, so I can use my points on any product I want.

To break it down: 500 points equals a $5 credit, 1,000 points gets you $10, and 1,500 points nets $15. Even better, there are easy ways to earn points without spending money. Signing up gets you 100 points, and submitting a review gets you 50 more. It might not sound like much, but if you’re already shopping, why not stack up a little credit for later? On top of that, rewards members get exclusive access to special sales and double-points events. Your loyalty points have an expiration date of 365 days after you earn them, so make sure to use them before they’re gone.

Save up to 25% Instantly With Dermstore Auto-Replenishment Discounts

Dermstore’s auto-replenishment subscription plan makes sense for beauty products you regularly use and run through quickly. You can save up to 25 percent off your first purchase and future refills, plus free shipping on recurring deliveries. It’s flexible, too, so you can adjust the delivery schedule, pause shipments, and cancel at any time with no commitment. I’d set it up for everyday essentials like lip balm and sunscreen, but there are plenty of options, including facial mists, oils, and cleansers. Note, though, that not every product online at Dermstore is eligible for subscription.

Select Customers Can Unlock a 15% Discount at Dermstore

Certain shoppers can get 15 percent off any Dermstore order, not just their first. Military members, veterans, teachers, first responders, and medical workers are eligible for this promotion; you’ll just need to undergo an online verification process to receive a unique discount code. Students are also eligible for the same 15 percent discount if they verify their status as well. Keep in mind that these codes aren’t stackable, so if you have another discount, you’ll need to choose one to use.

When you refer someone new to Dermstore, they’ll get the usual 15%off their first order, and you’ll score $50 in store credit. Highlighting Our Go-To Skincare Tech

Dermstore is known for its dermatologist-approved skincare, but don’t sleep on its beauty tools and devices.
